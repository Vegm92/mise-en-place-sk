## 2. Spotlight: Key Barcelona-Native Competitors

### haddock (Barcelona, 2020) — Most Direct Competitor
Founded in Barcelona; the closest existing product to mise-en-place. Uses AI/OCR to digitize invoices and albaranes via photo, auto-updates escandallo costs in real time. Backed by an Ingenico partnership. Used by high-profile Barcelona restaurants: **Estimar, Nandu Jubany's group, Isabella's group**.

Does NOT do supplier ordering or a procurement marketplace — purely cost intelligence and invoice digitization. Described in press as "the Spanish startup bringing AI to gastronomy."

### Gstock — Most Established Back-of-House Player
1,800+ hospitality licenses in Spain. Strong on escandallos (recipe costing with dynamic price recalculation), mobile inventory counting, OCR albarán reading, compliance (allergens, Verifactu). Acquired by PE-backed Everfield. Perceived as powerful but complex and expensive for small independents.

> "Pasamos de un 36-37% de coste de materia prima a un 33% en restaurantes gastronómicos." — Gstock user, GetApp

### Last.app — POS with Embedded Invoice Extraction
Spanish all-in-one POS that includes automated invoice extraction as a native feature (not an add-on). Integrates with haddock. Positioned as the modern POS for independents and groups. **Potential partner and competitor simultaneously.**

### Yurest Lite — Newest Direct Threat (Nov 2025)
Launched November 2025 specifically targeting independent single-venue hostelería. Features: supplier purchase orders, albarán digitization, OCR, purchase reporting, escandallos, AI recommendations. Claims 12% cost reduction in purchasing. **Still very new — narrow window to compete before they establish.**

### Controliza — AI-Positioned Procurement
AI demand forecasting, automated supplier ordering, inventory. 1,000+ restaurants including La Mafia se Sienta a la Mesa, Urban Poke. Claims 20% average profitability increase and 30% waste reduction after 3 months. Focused on chains/groups.

### Prezo — Focused Procurement Specialist
All-in-one: orders, invoices, inventory, recipes, price stats. OCR delivery note capture via photo. Growing SMB product. Verifactu-compliant. ~€79–149/month.

## 3. Real User Complaints & Pain Points

### Theme 1: Complexity and Steep Learning Curve

> "Los autónomos y pequeñas empresas hosteleras no perciben los beneficios que estas herramientas pueden aportar a sus negocios. Por el contrario, encuentran el aprendizaje de los mismos demasiado difícil y sienten que, más que ayudar en el desarrollo del negocio, pierden el tiempo." — Barcelona Culinary Hub / SEGITTUR Digitalization Study 2024

The 2024 SEGITTUR study found: over half of small hosteleros cite **lack of knowledge and training** as a primary barrier. **63% of restaurants have little to no awareness of available digitalization tools.**

### Theme 2: The Profitability Blind Spot

> "Solo el 41% de los restaurantes madrileños monitorizan su rentabilidad de forma semanal." — TheFork / Merca2.es study, April 2026

Spanish restaurants are increasingly digitalized at the front of house (reservations, POS, delivery) but remain blind on costs and margins. Back-of-house financial intelligence lags severely.

> "La principal barrera para mejorar la rentabilidad es la falta de herramientas, tiempo y formación para gestionar con precisión esta parte tan significativa del negocio." — Controliza blog

### Theme 3: Manual Invoice Processing Is the Core Pain

Every major competitor (haddock, Gstock, Prezo, Last.app, Yurest) prominently advertises OCR albarán/invoice capture as a key feature — strong market signal this is the pain point the market has converged on.

> "Valoramos mucho la posibilidad de cargar albaranes con una simple foto, y el OCR-i los incluye automáticamente al sistema actualizando los inventarios de producto." — Prezo user review

> "El sistema de inventario me ha reducido el tiempo dedicado a esta tarea un 75%." — Gstock user, GetApp

### Theme 4: Supplier Price Volatility Goes Undetected

Multiple platforms (Last.app, Gstock, haddock, Yurest) specifically advertise **automatic price-change alerts** as a core feature — reflecting real pain of suppliers quietly raising prices mid-invoice or between albarán cycles.

Yurest's research: **73% of Spanish restaurants lose ~€800/month per location** from undetected supplier price deviations.

### Theme 5: Fragmented Supplier Management

Spanish restaurant owners deal with many small local suppliers (Mercabarna produce, local meat, wine importers) with no unified ordering channel. Deliveristo, Piola App, and Camarero10's Central Compras module all try to solve this — but no tool integrates **order placement + invoice digitization + cost reconciliation** in one seamless loop.

### Theme 6: Enterprise Tools Too Expensive for SMBs

Gstock (€100–300+/month after PE acquisition) and Mapal/Easilys are too expensive or complex for independent single-location restaurants. The **63% of Spanish restaurants that remain undigitalized at back-of-house** represent a gap the mid-market hasn't filled.

### Theme 7: Regulatory Anxiety

Operators are anxious about Verifactu and e-invoicing deadlines but lack clear guidance. Several vendors (Powergest, Infosoft2000, Qamarero) have built Verifactu compliance as a marketing differentiator precisely because this anxiety is real.

## 4. Regulatory Landscape

### Verifactu (Real Decreto 1007/2023) — Mandatory for All

All invoicing software must be certified; every invoice cryptographically chained and transmitted real-time to AEAT, with a mandatory QR code on each invoice.

- **Sociedades (companies):** January 1, 2027
- **Autónomos and SMEs:** July 1, 2027
- **Sanctions:** Up to €50,000/year for using non-certified software; up to €150,000/year for software manufacturers
- **Cost of adaptation:** €1,500–€8,000 per establishment

Any invoice management tool operating in Spain must be Verifactu-compliant before these dates. **This is mandatory table stakes.**

### Ley Crea y Crece — B2B Electronic Invoicing (Real Decreto 238/2026)

Mandates electronic invoicing for all B2B transactions. Applies to **invoices received from suppliers** (not customer restaurant receipts).

- Companies billing €8M+ annually: **October 1, 2026**
- All other companies and autónomos: **October 2027–2028**

Supplier invoices will flow through structured electronic channels (FacturaE, Peppol). Direct tailwind for any procurement software handling supplier invoicing — and opportunity to move beyond OCR into direct API ingestion.

### TicketBAI (Basque Country only)

Mandatory for all hospitality in Gipuzkoa (Nov 2022), Álava (2022), Bizkaia (Jul 2025). Separate certification from Verifactu. Relevant only for Spain-wide rollout beyond Catalonia.

### Compliance Calendar

| Regulation | What It Covers | Who | Key Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verifactu | Outgoing invoice certification | All restaurants | Jan/Jul 2027 |
| Ley Crea y Crece | Incoming B2B e-invoices (supplier invoices) | All restaurants | Oct 2026–2028 |
| TicketBAI | Sales ticket chaining | Basque Country only | Already live |
| SII | Real-time VAT ledger reporting | Large operators (€6M+) only | Already live |

### Kit Digital (Demand Driver Opportunity)

Government subsidies of **€2,000–€12,000** to SMBs for digital tools. Becoming a certified **Agente Digitalizador** means restaurants can acquire mise-en-place at zero direct cost. How Qamarero and Tipsi are growing. Program closed for new applications October 2025 — existing beneficiaries executing 2026 projects are an immediate warm-audience funnel.

## 6. Gaps & Opportunities

### Gap 1: No Tool Closes the Full Purchase-to-Payment Loop for SMBs
The market has tools that let you order from suppliers, tools that digitize invoices, and tools that track food cost — but **no single affordable, SMB-friendly tool** connects: `supplier catalog → order placement → albarán capture → invoice reconciliation → cost-per-dish update → payment tracking → regulatory compliance (Verifactu + e-invoicing)`.

### Gap 2: Supplier Price Intelligence Is Fragmented
All major players alert owners when a supplier changes a price, but none provides cross-market benchmarking ("you're paying 18% above what other restaurants pay this supplier for this ingredient category"). AI could surface this intelligently.

### Gap 3: Regulatory Compliance Is Urgent and Underserved for Independent Restaurants
Only 15–20% of small independents are actively preparing for Verifactu or Ley Crea y Crece. The 2027 deadlines are close enough to create urgency. A tool that frames itself as "your Verifactu-ready procurement solution" addresses a regulatory anxiety few tools have fully claimed for the SMB segment.

### Gap 4: Spanish-Language AI That Understands Local Document Formats
Foreign tools (MarketMan, Apicbase) have weak Spanish localization. Spanish albaranes, facturas, and notas de entrega have specific formats, IVA breakdowns, and nomenclature that generic OCR handles poorly. A Spain-native AI that correctly extracts IVA, número de factura, CIF, and product line items from Spanish supplier documents is meaningfully better.

### Gap 5: Affordable Tier for Micro-Restaurants
Gstock and Apicbase are mid-to-enterprise-priced (€100–300+/month). haddock prices are undisclosed but positioned premium. Room for a tool at **€29–59/month** that delivers 80% of the value for single-location independent restaurants — which represent 65%+ of the Spanish market.

### Gap 6: The "Digitalization Without Finance" Paradox
Only 41% of Spanish restaurants monitor profitability weekly. The data exists (in POS and invoices) but the financial intelligence layer is missing. An AI tool that auto-generates margin insights from invoice and POS data with zero manual work by the owner directly addresses this gap.

## 7. Differentiation Recommendations

### 1. Own the "Invoice to Insight" Narrative
haddock's positioning is "scan your invoice, know your food cost." Mise-en-place must go further: **"Scan your invoice, know your food cost, know you're compliant with Verifactu, and know when your supplier raised their price without telling you."** The AI should auto-narrate: "Your tomato cost rose 12% last month — your bruschetta margin is now 3% below target." This turns invoice scanning from data entry into an intelligence tool.

### 2. Make Verifactu Compliance a Launch-Time Feature, Not a Roadmap Item
A procurement tool that is Verifactu-certified on day one and handles structured B2B e-invoice receipt under Ley Crea y Crece has a built-in sales argument no competitor has fully claimed for the SMB procurement segment. Message: "The law is changing. You need compliant invoicing. Here's a tool that does compliance AND saves you money."

### 3. Win Barcelona by Partnering With the Mercabarna Ecosystem
Barcelona's restaurant supply chain flows heavily through Mercabarna. Building catalog integrations with 10–15 key Mercabarna-linked suppliers (produce, fish, meat) would instantly make mise-en-place's ordering feature meaningful for Barcelona restaurants. Use Barcelona Culinary Hub, Hostelco, and Forward Fooding Hub as distribution channels.

### 4. Target the Barcelona Premium Independent Tier First
haddock's success with Estimar and Isabella's group validates the strategy: sign 5–10 high-profile Barcelona independent restaurants (not necessarily Michelin stars — well-known independent groups with 2–5 locations). These restaurants are influential, pay for quality, and are vocal about tools they use.

### 5. Price at €49–79/month for Single-Location Restaurants
Directly undercuts Gstock (€100–300/month post-PE) and positions above Piola App (near-free, limited). The €49–79 range is "a bottle of wine per month" for a Barcelona restaurateur — low enough the conversation is about value, not budget.

### 6. Build the Supplier Portal That None of the Others Have
A lightweight portal that suppliers submit digital invoices (albaranes electrónicos) directly into the restaurant's account. This flips the paradigm from "restaurant scans the paper invoice" to "supplier sends structured data, restaurant confirms." Given Ley Crea y Crece mandates B2B e-invoicing, this is the direction the market must go — mise-en-place can be ahead of the curve.

### 7. Compete on Simplicity, Not Feature Count
The 2024 SEGITTUR study shows the #1 barrier to digitalization is perceived complexity. Message: "Set up in 15 minutes. Works from your phone. Takes a photo, does the rest." Emphasize onboarding speed (minutes, not days) and zero-training-required design. Deliberate contrast to Gstock (powerful but complex) and Mapal (enterprise, overtly complex).

### 8. Spanish-First AI as a Trust Signal
English-language tools are distrusted in Spain's SMB hospitality market. The AI must speak Spanish fluently, understand Spanish invoice formats, handle IVA reducido vs. IVA superreducido for food products, and handle Catalan regional document variants for Barcelona. Frame explicitly: "Construido en Barcelona, para restaurantes españoles."

### 9. POS Integrations to Prioritize
- **Revo XEF** — strong in Barcelona independent restaurants
- **Ágora POS** — 37,000 Spanish clients
- **Last.app** — fast-growing modern POS with existing invoice features (potential partner and competitor — evaluate carefully; haddock already integrated with them)
- **Square** — haddock's existing POS partner
