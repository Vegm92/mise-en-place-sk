## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints, the failure mode being avoided, the
thing that stopped working. Enough that someone who has never seen this code
understands why doing nothing was not an option.

Name the alternatives here, with the reason each was rejected — that is the part
readers come back for.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense. Include the concrete shape: the function,
the table, the state machine, the threshold and where its number came from.

## Consequences

What the team now lives with — the good, the costs, and the parts that are worse
than the alternative would have been. Be specific about what is *not* handled:
an ADR that reads as unambiguously correct is usually hiding something.

Include operational consequences (an env var that must be set, a job that must
run) and the tests or CI gates that hold the decision in place.
