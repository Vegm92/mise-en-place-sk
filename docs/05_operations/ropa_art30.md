# GDPR Article 30 — Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)

Derived from the running code, schema and existing docs, not drafted from scratch. Every
claim below cites a path. `**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**` marks a fact this repo cannot supply
(legal entity, DPO, retention decisions, executed DPAs) — a one-line note says what's needed.
Backbone: `src/lib/server/tenant-data-map.ts` (the #390 export/deletion traversal already
used by `api/user/export` and `api/user/delete`) plus every non-tenant-scoped table in
`src/lib/server/schema.ts`.

**Controller (Art. 30(1)(a), all activities): `**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**`.** Privacy policy
and terms name "Mise en Place SL" as controller (`src/routes/privacy/+page.svelte:17`,
`src/routes/terms/+page.svelte:36`), but no such entity is registered anywhere in this repo
— per **#779** there is no incorporated legal entity today. Registered address and DPO
contact are equally unset. Nothing below can name a controller until #779 resolves.

## Findings surfaced while compiling this record (one line each)

- Controller identity is `**OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**` across every row — no legal entity exists to name (#779).
- Hosting region (`asia-southeast1`, all three services incl. Postgres) is a Chapter V transfer the privacy policy doesn't disclose as such — it labels it "Southeast Asia" under the Railway row without naming a transfer safeguard (#978).
- AWS S3, Resend and the WhatsApp/Meta channel process personal data but are absent from the privacy policy's sub-processor table entirely (#784).
- The account-deletion "30 days for reversal" the privacy policy promises does not exist in code — deletion is immediate and synchronous (`api/user/delete/+server.ts`).
- No automated purge enforces the disclosed 5-year (invoices/audit log) or 90-day (session/logs) retention periods.
